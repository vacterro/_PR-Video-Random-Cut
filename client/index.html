How this works

Pick a bin of footage, mark a zone on your timeline, hit Randomize. The tool cuts random pieces from the bin and scatters them across your chosen tracks.

  1. Put your footage in a bin (e.g. "Content") in this project, then pick it in Source.
  2. Set a work area (In/Out) on the sequence you want to fill.
  3. Hover any "?" — every control explains what it does and when to use it.
  4. Click Refresh after adding clips, tracks, or markers so the panel sees them.
  5. Click "Randomize & Place". Don't like it? "Reroll All" redoes everything.
  6. One piece bothers you? Select it in the timeline and click "Replace Selected" — same spot, same length, new content.
  7. Voiceover workflow: set your voice track in section 8, then "Fill Narrator Gaps" covers only the silent pauses.
  8. Click a section title to collapse it once configured — settings still apply.

Undo

Every run is one undo step — Ctrl+Z reverts the whole thing like any other edit.

If something goes red

If track auto-create fails on your Premiere version, add the tracks manually in the timeline and run again.

Clip Weights & Limits

Increase a clip's weight to make it appear more often. Weight 2.0 means it is picked twice as often as 1.0.
